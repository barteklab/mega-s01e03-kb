  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants get day and month swapped: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.”
  • hint: booking-v3’s date field prints MM/DD/YYYY. At day-first tenants people type the month first, as told, and the form reads the day first. Only form bookings at booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not reach it: the form already reads the tenant’s way.
  • one-day: the booking lands one day off, not swapped. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” That is a time-zone shift, not a format. The desk flagged these in its notes, but the #4471 page still counts them under the parser.

How the reports were placed

By the tenant, not by intake’s tag. Intake guesses the cause from the reporter’s browser; the cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts). The two disagree in both directions on this ticket. The ticket export also repeats some reports; each id counts once.

Why one ticket hid three

Triage merges on subject line alone (decision 0001). Every one of these reports has a date-sounding subject (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Dates mixed up”), so they all merged into the first open date ticket, whatever the body said. The body is where the problems differ.
